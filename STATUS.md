# Research status — 28 October 2026

**The corrected three-axiom classification and critical percolation universality remain open.** Locality compares common colored source neighborhoods, including different targets. The historical target-germ example does not satisfy this corrected axiom.

## Results from the current continuation

- **Canonical restart compatibility follows from genuine strong DMP.** For a Borel continuous-driver family, the regular-conditional-law restart identity in the usual natural filtration transports its continuous canonical drift and bracket automatically. A separate characteristic-compatibility axiom is unnecessary. Conditional kernels on random slit states still have to exist. Pointwise convergence of continuous deterministic-state kernels is insufficient; a moving-spike example proves this, while an annealed error criterion on visited states suffices. See sec:canonical-restart-automatic and prop:automatic-characteristics.
- **Microscopic correctors can replace raw one-step bias bounds.** An adapted corrector with E sup|R|→0 and controlled signed conditional capacity increments gives the same bounded-drift continuum limit. A finite-state Poisson equation is one sufficient construction, without claiming such a closure for percolation. Bounded approximants of stopped Brownian motion can have raw one-step bias constants diverging to infinity. See prop:corrector-bias-transfer.
- **Random local coordinate maps no longer obstruct ordered-germ tensor decoding under the stated model assumptions.** Whole spherical empirical orbit measures give one conull set valid simultaneously under every affine map and every local C1 diffeomorphism, even if sample dependent. The stationary cylinder driver is ergodic under every fixed time shift, giving full dilation ergodicity for the whole-plane SLE model. With explicit proper-trace/Borel-coding premises, this decodes an affine tensor at deterministic capacity tips of simple SLEκ, κ<4. It does not select an unlabelled frontier point or cover all stopping times. See thm:wp-robust-decoder, prop:wp-cylinder-ergodic, and (WP12).
- **Annular fluctuations admit a martingale description.** The existing exponential absolute regularity gives an exact nonstationary martingale/corrector decomposition, uniformly exponential corrector moments, and O(log N) maximal corrector almost surely. A deterministic asymptotic variance rate yields a functional CLT; fixed-lag Cesàro covariance limits suffice for that rate. Neither scale expectations nor variance convergence are derived for general percolation. See prop:annular-martingale and thm:annular-clt.
- **Near-critical germ singularity forces an energy obstruction.** Aumann's 2014 theorem separates distinct near-critical interface parameters already on the raw initial ordered germ and supplies a curve-level dilation obstruction under its convergence hypotheses. A localized Girsanov argument proves that finite-energy Brownian driving drift preserves Wiener germ probabilities. If a nonzero near-critical limit has a Brownian-bracket-six plus absolutely continuous drift representation, its drift energy must be infinite on every positive initial interval. This is not a nonsemimartingality theorem or a construction of a coherent strong-DMP family. See sec:germ-energy.

- **An intrinsic measure class survives affine deformation.** For compact model traces with finite nonzero local Minkowski content, a Borel tube-cluster construction selects a measure with exact similarity covariance. On every fixed affine image it has precisely the null sets of the transported model content, even without convergence of the affine image's tube measures. Gaussian-energy minimization makes the selector canonical. See thm:intrinsic-tube-measure and cor:intrinsic-point-decoder.

- **An affine tensor is recoverable from unlabelled compact simple-SLE subarcs.** Whole-plane SLEκ(2) reversibility, the stationary cylinder, and the natural-length Palm identity give the correct model at Minkowski-content-typical points for 0<κ<4. The intrinsic measure, a Borel two-orientation selector, and weak tube convergence then yield one Borel decoder from the compact trace alone. On one conull event it works simultaneously for every affine map and every nontrivial compact interior subarc, including sample-dependent choices. This addresses natural-point selection for known simple-SLE pieces; it does not identify unknown exploration-frontier laws. See thm:natural-point-tensor and thm:unlabelled-simple-arc.

## Earlier results retained

Controlled conditional first-moment bounds give bounded canonical drift; mirrored shrinking slits then force zero initial drift and bracket tangent six. Genuine strong DMP propagates these to SLE₆. Discrete signed capacity-bias bounds, or tested block estimates, transfer without filtration convergence. The shrinking-slit constants must satisfy εKε→0; this is not supplied by RSW. The new corrector criterion is an alternative way to establish the same bound.

Independent-color RSW shields give source-patch coalescence, an initial ordered-germ zero-one law, total-variation prefix/distant-suffix decoupling, and exponential annular mixing with a centered strong law, without dilation invariance or limiting DMP. Regular endpoint tensor decoding and exact suffix/source splicing collapse coherent affine-SLE₆ mixtures to one deterministic common tensor. The affine-mixture representation remains unproved. Earlier driving criteria allow finite-variation initial tangents or tight random tangents; a wrong affine structure cannot be tame in all bounded domains.

## Bottlenecks and next steps

1. Construct a percolation driving corrector, or prove signed tested-block cancellation, in one unknown fixed affine normalization. Control the corrected bias constants in shrinking slit domains. RSW and Brownian-order absolute increments do not provide signed cancellation.
2. Prove compatible strong continuation kernels on random slit domains, possibly through the new annealed criterion. Deterministic-domain marginal convergence, even with continuous individual kernels, is insufficient.
3. For the abstract rough-frontier route, identify accessible frontier pieces with suitable simple-SLE laws and extract those pieces measurably from the domain. Intrinsic measure, natural-point tensor decoding, and unlabelled subarc decoding are now proved for known simple-SLE pieces (0<κ<4). This does not prove those model laws for the unknown family or compatibility of a domain-label construction at every random slit state. Capacity-typical and natural-length-typical points have different local models.
4. Derive affine-SLE/affine-mixture marginals or identify deterministic scale expectations from coarse geometry. The annular Gaussian theorem isolates a variance limit but does not prove it. Whole-slit perturbation stability still lacks the deterministic implication from ordered disagreement to a boundary-arm event; tip shields leave altered boundary behind them.

No unconditional near-critical counterexample is claimed. Its ordered-kernel coherence remains separate from known curve-germ singularity. Brownian full support does not repair null-history gaps.
